Quality & downloads

Every download saves an MP4 with sound and a separate 320 kbps MP3. The best available video and audio are fetched first. Video is copied into MP4 when possible; incompatible codecs are converted while preserving resolution and frame rate.

DRM, site blocks, or unsupported players can prevent a download. Right-click a video or use its individual post page when a feed does not expose a permalink. Keep yt-dlp current by running Update.command.

No analytics, advertising, or cloud service. Recent download status stays in this browser session. MIT-licensed source.
